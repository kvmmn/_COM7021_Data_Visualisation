## Contrast (WCAG 2.2, measured)

| Pair | Ratio | Target | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| ink on paper | 13.90:1 | 7:1 | pass |
| mute on paper | 7.00:1 | 4.5:1 | pass |
| white on ink | 15.54:1 | 7:1 | pass |
| white on accent_deep | 8.29:1 | 4.5:1 | pass |
| white on accent | 3.85:1 | 4.5:1 | fail (optional) |

Mute was kept dark enough that ticks do not vanish on bone. Oxide `accent` is a mark, not a chip.

## Psychology

- Bone and vellum: historical document, long reading, low glare.
- Oxide / iron: aged instrument, not rust-as-error.
- The highlight should feel like a marginalia mark in the gutter, not a traffic light.

## Sequential / categorical

Five earths only. Sequential ramps stay umber-to-bone. Do not add a sky blue “for variety”.

## Refusals

Coffee-shop brown gradients, terracotta SaaS, using oxide as a global brand fill. One highlighted bar is enough.
