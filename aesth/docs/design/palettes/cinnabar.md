**Status:** frozen  
**Proofs:** `studies/palette_poster.png`, `studies/anscombe_cinnabar.png`

Graphite with a rare earth-red claim. For exceptions, delay, risk — one alarm, not a red dashboard.

This is the rare palette where `accent` itself may carry white chip text.

## Psychology

- Vermilion / cinnabar historically marks the important note, not the page.
- Too much red becomes hazard tape. `accent` is for the claimed bars / exception strip only.
- Structure stays graphite so red cannot also be “brand”.

## Sequential / categorical

Never colour-only warnings. Pair the red bar with position and a label. Deuteranomaly still separates graphite from cinnabar better than red-from-green, but the geometry must do the job.

## Refusals

RGB `#FF0000`, coral SaaS, red-everywhere KPI walls, a second orange “for severity”.
