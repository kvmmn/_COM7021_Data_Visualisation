`accent` is dark enough for white chips and still reads as stain, not as a visited hyperlink.

## Psychology

- Iodine tincture: empirical, slightly medicinal, rare in dashboards — distinctive.
- Brown-violet stays earthbound; electric purple reads as decoration.
- Use as a sophisticated categorical family, not as a nightclub accent.

## Sequential / categorical

Five stops mix umber and dried violet so adjacent series do not collapse. Do not add a second lilac.

## Refusals

`#7C3AED` and friends, neon purple, ultraviolet glow, “AI” violet gradients.
