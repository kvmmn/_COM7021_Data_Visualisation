# Palette: `chloris`

**Status:** frozen  
**Proofs:** `studies/palette_poster.png`

Olive, moss, dry leaf. Botanical without “eco green” cliché.

Olive `accent` is a fill. White text uses moss `accent_deep`.

## Psychology

- Chloris (green vegetation) as cultivated landscape, not recycling-bin green.
- Olive reads older and quieter than lime.
- The highlight is a deeper moss, not a saturated leaf.

## Sequential / categorical

Keep the family inside olive–moss. A mobility KPI (for example CO₂ saved) can use `accent` as a bar fill without looking like a climate campaign poster.

## Refusals

Yellow-green, lime, recycling-arrow green, leaf icons as decoration.
